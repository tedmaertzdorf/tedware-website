01

Introduction

Tedware respects the privacy of people who use its websites, applications and games. This policy should explain which product it covers, what information that product processes and the choices available to users.

Action required: identify the exact app, game, website or service covered by this version of the policy and name the responsible business entity.

02

Information We Collect

This section must describe only information the relevant product actually collects or receives. This may include information provided directly by a user, technical information, gameplay or app activity, and information received through third parties—but only where those categories are factually applicable.

Action required: document each data category, its source and whether providing it is optional. If no personal information is collected, confirm that after a technical audit and state it precisely.

03

How We Use Information

Explain the specific purpose for every data category listed above, such as providing a requested feature, maintaining security or responding to support requests. Do not list a purpose unless the product genuinely uses information for that purpose.

Action required: map every data category to a clear purpose and, where required, an appropriate legal basis.

04

Third-Party Services

List external services, software development kits, hosting providers or platforms that receive or process information in connection with the product. Include links to their privacy information where appropriate.

Action required: audit the production build and list every applicable third party—or explicitly state that none are used after this has been verified. Do not assume analytics, advertising or tracking is present.

05

Data Retention

Describe how long each type of personal information is kept, or explain the criteria used to determine that period. Also explain what happens when information is no longer needed.

Action required: define and verify retention periods, deletion routines and any legally required exceptions.

06

Children's Privacy

Explain whether the product is directed to children and which minimum age or consent rules apply in the markets where it is offered. The wording must match the product's real audience, store listing and data practices.

Action required: confirm the intended audience, age rating, distribution countries and any parental-consent process before choosing final wording.

07

Security

Describe reasonable safeguards used to protect information while acknowledging that no method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

Action required: document safeguards that are actually implemented and avoid absolute security guarantees.

08

User Rights

Explain the privacy rights available to users, which may include access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection or data portability depending on applicable law and the circumstances.

Action required: confirm applicable jurisdictions, the rights request process, identity-verification steps and the contact channel.

09

Changes to This Privacy Policy

State how policy updates will be published and how users will be informed when a change is material. Keep the effective date at the top of this page current.

Action required: choose an update notification method that fits the product and its users.

10

Contact

Provide a working channel for privacy questions and rights requests, along with any legally required business contact details.
