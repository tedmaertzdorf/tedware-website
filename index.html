About Tedware

Small by design. Serious about the work.

Tedware is a Dutch sole proprietorship focused on developing software, mobile applications, games and digital products.

As an independent studio, Tedware can stay close to both the technical details and the experience of the people using the product. The aim is straightforward: build useful, well-considered software without unnecessary complexity or inflated promises.
