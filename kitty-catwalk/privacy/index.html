At a glance

Fashion shows run offline, with simulated contestants. Progress and the Paw Coin wallet are stored on your device. Optional Apple purchases need an internet connection. Game cloud sync is currently disabled. This game has no player chat or game account sign-in.

Who is responsible?

Ted Maertzdorf operates Kitty Catwalk: Dress Up under the Tedware brand and is responsible for the personal information he receives through support. Tedware is a brand, not a separate incorporated legal entity. For support or privacy questions, email support.tedware@gmail.com. Ted Maertzdorf manages this mailbox.

What stays on your device

The game saves levels, XP, world progression, match and reward history, achievements, quests, owned clothing and cosmetics, saved outfits, tutorial progress, sound and motion settings, and Paw Coin credits and spending. A random profile identifier associates these records. Saves also include operation identifiers, revisions, timestamps, recovery copies and purchase review flags. This supports consistent rewards, ownership and recovery after interruptions.

Apple purchases

Apple processes payments and supplies localized product prices and transaction information through StoreKit and Unity In-App Purchasing 5.3.1. The game sends a randomly generated game profile token to Apple to associate a purchase with the correct profile. It checks signed transaction information and stores transaction and product IDs, the Sandbox or Production environment, profile association, coin amounts, purchase/grant/confirmation times and review status. Apple handles your Apple Account and payment details; the game does not request your Apple password or full card number. Full signed transaction payloads are used for verification and are not written to the game’s own pending-purchase journal.

Local saves and recovery limits

There is no active game CloudKit sync or independent financial backend in this release configuration. Local recovery copies may help with interrupted saves when valid evidence remains. Apple may redeliver an unfinished purchase. Finished consumable coin packs do not provide a reliable record of your remaining balance. Deleting the app, losing a device or moving to another device can lose progress, coins and ownership. An iOS device backup may include app data, depending on your settings; it is separate from game cloud sync and is not a guarantee of current-balance recovery. Support cannot promise to reconstruct missing or already-spent coins.

Services, diagnostics and tracking

The current game configuration uses Unity for gameplay and Unity IAP to communicate with Apple’s purchase service. Advertising SDKs, gameplay analytics, Unity cloud diagnostics and crash reporting are disabled. The game does not implement advertising tracking or request access to location, contacts, camera or microphone. Apple may separately process App Store, backup and diagnostic information according to your Apple settings and its policies.

When you contact support

Email support.tedware@gmail.com for purchase, progress or privacy questions. Your email provider and Gmail process delivery. Ted Maertzdorf receives the sender address, message and any attachments you choose to send. Share only what is needed: app version, device model, iOS version, approximate issue time and a description. If needed, send a redacted Apple purchase reference; remove unrelated purchases and personal or payment details. Never send passwords, full card numbers or a device backup. Support is optional and does not subscribe you to marketing.

We use support information to answer your request, investigate a reported problem and handle purchase or privacy questions. Where applicable, this is necessary to provide support connected with your use or purchase of the game, or serves our legitimate interests in resolving requests and preventing duplicate or fraudulent claims, balanced against your privacy rights. Information necessary to meet a legal obligation is processed on that basis. We do not use support messages for advertising or sell them.

Visiting this website

tedware.nl is hosted on GitHub Pages. The hosting provider receives your IP address and ordinary browser and request information when you visit. These pages add no analytics, advertising trackers or contact form. The EN/NL selector stores only your language preference in your browser under tedware-language; clear the website’s browser data to remove it. Apple, Google/Gmail and GitHub process information under their own privacy policies. These providers may process information outside your country, including in the United States. Their linked policies explain their transfer safeguards, including applicable adequacy frameworks and standard contractual clauses. We do not claim that support email or hosting is stored only in the EU.

Retention and deletion

Local game records and recovery evidence remain while the app data is on your device. Financial history is not automatically pruned because it helps prevent duplicate grants and unsafe recovery of spent coins. There is no in-game permanent erasure or cloud deletion workflow. Removing app data can remove progress and coins; device backups may retain separate copies managed through your device or Apple settings. Do not delete the app as a first troubleshooting step. Contact support.tedware@gmail.com with questions about support-message retention or to request deletion. Provider-managed backups and Apple’s records follow their own retention rules.

Children and families

Kitty Catwalk is a casual fashion game for a general audience, including families. A parent or guardian should handle purchases, support and privacy requests for younger players. The game has no player chat, public sharing, advertising or game account registration, and does not collect age, date of birth or a parental-consent record. Please do not send unnecessary information about a child. If such information is received through support, contact us so it can be reviewed and unnecessary information deleted. Apple’s purchase confirmation, Ask to Buy and Screen Time restrictions depend on your device and family settings.

Privacy requests and security

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information, restrict or object to processing, obtain a copy, or complain to a data protection authority. Contact the support address for requests about information received through support. We cannot remotely inspect or erase your device’s local save or Apple’s records. The game checks transaction identity and uses durable local records and recovery safeguards, but a local save is not a trusted server or a guarantee against a modified device. Never send your complete financial save to a public forum.
